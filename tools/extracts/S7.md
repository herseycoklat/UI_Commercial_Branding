- Two boxes shown from your pages, three you write, one optional. The number is written before the test, never after.
- CORE PAGE

- FOR WHOM · SESSION 2, PAGE 4 · SHOWN, NOT RETYPED
- Shown from Session 2, page 4: the FOR WHOM row of your card.
- WHO WE ASK
- — people who match FOR WHOM. Not your team, not your friends. How many? —
- WHAT WE ASK THEM TO DO · DO OR PAY
- — one action on the DO or PAY rung. Usually your WHAT WE ASK YOU TO DO row; if that cannot prove the bet wrong, choose one that can, and write the gap here —
- OUR PREDICTION · BEFORE THE TEST
- — "___ of ___ will ___." Written before the test —
- RESULT (OPTIONAL)
- — optional. "Not tested yet" is a valid answer. A class result is labelled practice —
- ONE LADDER   SAY what people tell you.  DO a small step that is easy to undo: a click, a sign-up, a free event, a refundable deposit.  PAY giving something they would lose if they back out: money, time they booked, a phone number to be contacted, a recommendation.
- CARD · ROW 9  Row 9 of your card, WHAT WE'RE BETTING ON, is the bet on this page. It is for the team: don't show it to the person you test.
- Common mistake: asking people whether they like it. That is SAY, and it tells you they are polite. Ask them to do something. Asking classmates and calling it evidence: a class test is practice; your classmates are not your FOR WHOM.

- STRONG FINDING OR WEAK SIGNAL
- Strong: people did or paid something, they are outside your team, and the pattern showed up more than once. Weak: only words, one source, friends, or a class practice test.
- Common mistake: a finding without "how many out of how many". A sheet where every row is strong is almost always wrong; a sheet where every row is weak is a finding about your evidence.

## Slide 4
- 4
- Strategy Revision Log
- One row per change. No evidence, no row.
- CORE PAGE
- TEAM WORKBOOK
- What this page answers: what you changed because of evidence, and how strong that evidence is.
| LINE · WHERE IT LIVES | BEFORE | AFTER | EVIDENCE | STRONG / WEAK |
| — the line, and the page it lives on — | — the old line — | — the new line — | — what made you change it: class test, weekly feedback, other teams' cards, your own test, or the page 9 check (always WEAK) — | — STRONG / WEAK — |
|  |  |  |  |  |
|  |  |  |  |  |
- STRONG FINDING OR WEAK SIGNAL
- A small change (wording, an added fact on the card) may come from a weak signal, if the row says WEAK. A big change (audience, positioning, price) needs a strong finding.
- REJECTED COUNTS THE SAME AS SUPPORTED
- A result that proved your bet wrong tells you what to stop doing. A row that says "we were wrong, so we changed it" is the most professional row on the page.
- Common mistake: a row with no evidence, or evidence that is really a preference. Changing the positioning because of one comment. Zero rows: if nothing changed, either nothing was tested or nothing was heard. A fix found by the page 9 check, or your own reasoning, is WEAK; a big change with no strong finding goes in the page 9 box.

Naming Evaluation Matrix

Score 1–5 on every criterion, with one line of reason. A name that is already locked still gets scored.

MEANINGFUL

1 2 3 4 5

Does it support the positioning?

DISTINCTIVE & FINDABLE

1 2 3 4 5

Does it stand out — and can it be searched for?

PRONOUNCEABLE

1 2 3 4 5

Can your audience say it and spell it after hearing it once?

PROTECTABLE

1 2 3 4 5

Could this realistically be registered?

ROOM TO GROW

1 2 3 4 5

Still true with a sequel, spin-off, or merchandise?

MITIGATION FOR EVERY SCORE OF 1–2
If the name can't change, what will you do instead? (Register a stylised mark rather than the word · always lock the wordmark to another element · secure a qualified handle · budget for paid search)

A low score you cannot fix is exactly what justifies paying to fix it another way. Scoring your own locked name 4s and 5s produces nothing usable.

## Slide 5

5

Creative Brand Brief

The core artefact. Written for a designer who has never met your team.

OBJECTIVE
What do you need made, and what must it achieve?

AUDIENCE
Who is it for? Straight from Session 2.

POSITIONING TO EXPRESS
Your Session 3 sentence. Which part must the identity carry?

PERSONALITY & TONE OF VOICE
Write pairs: “warm, not sweet”. Add one right and one wrong sentence.

MANDATORY ELEMENTS
Requirements, not solutions. Things that must be true of any answer.

PROHIBITED ELEMENTS
What must NOT appear — and the strategic reason why.

ACCESSIBILITY REQUIREMENTS
Contrast, thumbnail legibility, never colour alone, captions.

A LINE WE REJECTED → REWROTE
Keep the dictating version visible next to the fix.

Test every line: is it a requirement, or a solution wearing a requirement's clothes? Could a stranger act on this without one spoken conversation?

## Slide 6

6

Brand Codes Inventory

Maximum five. The elements that identify you with the logo deleted.

CODES WE ALREADY HAVE
Colour, shape, sound, typography, character. Only the ones that genuinely repeat.

CODES WE STILL NEED
What's missing? Write UNKNOWN where nothing exists yet — that's a gap, not an omission.

OWNER OF EACH CODE
Who approves a change to it? A code with no owner drifts within a month.

THE NO-LOGO TEST
For each code: would this still identify us if the logo were deleted? Cross out everything that fails.

Expect this page to get SHORTER as you work. A team that ends with five strong codes on the first pass usually hasn't applied the no-logo test at all.

## Slide 7

7

Multisensory Identity Map

Six channels. Where one isn't needed, write “not needed, because…” — never blank.

VISUAL
Character, palette, wordmark, layout, application
Needed? Who makes it? When?

VERBAL
Tone in trailers, captions, press, in-product copy
Needed? Who makes it? When?

SONIC
A motif, ident, or signature sound — separate from the score
Needed? Who makes it? When?

MOTION
How things move, appear, and transition
Needed? Who makes it? When?

SPATIAL
Venues, lobbies, booths, events, screenings
Needed? Who makes it? When?

INTERACTIVE / BEHAVIOURAL
App, game, service behaviour, how you reply to people
Needed? Who makes it? When?

Filling all six enthusiastically is usually wishful thinking rather than ambition. Name who makes each one and when — that's what separates a plan from a wish.

## Slide 3


3

Points of Parity & Points of Difference

Derive both columns from your Session 2 Competitor Matrix — not imagination.

WHAT WE MUST MATCH (parity)

The entry ticket — what you must be good at just to be taken seriously. Don't leave this empty.

WHERE WE WIN (difference)

Why they pick you over the alternatives — the thing only you can credibly claim.

First be good enough. THEN be different. A beautifully different product that fails the basics never gets a chance to be different.


## Slide 4


4

Brand Platform Canvas

Eight boxes. Each 1–2 sentences — if it needs a paragraph, it isn't distilled yet.

PURPOSE

Why do we deserve to exist, beyond profit? What does it make us REFUSE to do?

VISION

The future state we're working toward. Rarely changes.

MISSION

What we DO, day to day, to move toward that vision.

VALUES

Principles that guide us — even when inconvenient. What would cost us something to keep?

ESSENCE

The brand in 2–4 words. Internal use only.

PROMISE

The one thing the audience can always expect from us.

PERSONALITY

If the brand walked in as a person — how would they talk?

REASONS TO BELIEVE

The proof that makes the promise credible.

Coherence check: pick any two boxes at random — can you explain how they connect?

## Slide 2

2

Evidence Review

Sort what you gathered since Session 1. Be brutal — most first drafts are mostly guesses.

FACT
Source + number, always.
Something you observed or someone told you directly. Write it as “9 of 14 people said X”, not as a conclusion about why.

INTERPRETATION
Your reasonable reading of a fact — still needs testing.

GUESS
What you assume or hope is true, with no evidence yet.

Each claim goes into exactly ONE column — not the same claim walked across all three. A claim with no named source and no number is never a FACT, however confidently it was said out loud. An empty FACT column is honest: write 0 as a fact, e.g. “0 interviews so far.”

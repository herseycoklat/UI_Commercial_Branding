## Slide 4

- Pricing Assumption Sheet
- 4
- CORE PAGE
- TEAM WORKBOOK
- Every figure carries its origin. Separate the prices you decide from the prices someone else decides.
- What this page checks: is your price compared with what people already pay, and does one customer bring in more than they cost you?
- WHAT IS PRICED / THE FIGURE / REFERENCE PRICE / VOLUME ASSUMED / ORIGIN: MEASURED / COMPARABLE / GUESS / UNKNOWN
- — a ticket, a unit, a package, a sponsor slot — / — your price, or "set by ___" if it isn't yours — / — the price already in your audience's head (the HTML tool shows your S2 Competitor Matrix Price row) — / — how many, over what period — / — MEASURED, COMPARABLE (name it), GUESS, or UNKNOWN + action —
- OUR PRICE AGAINST THE REFERENCE: ABOVE / AT / BELOW, BECAUSE… / Our price is ABOVE / AT / BELOW the reference, because ______ (from your Session 3 positioning). Not paid with money? Write what they give instead: time, attention, an email, a recommendation.
- THE SURVIVAL LINE · ONE CUSTOMER, ONE RECEIPT / Money one customer brings  −  cost to win and serve that one customer  =  ______.  Negative is normal this early; not knowing is not. If a piece is unknowable today, write which piece and why.
- No naked numbers. A GUESS is not a failure: it makes the row a candidate for page 8 (your three bets). A sheet where every origin says MEASURED is almost always wrong.

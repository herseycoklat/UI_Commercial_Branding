THE HOURS TEST
Total MUST hours ÷ number of people = ____ hours per person per week. Above about 20, before other classes? Cut.
WHAT WE CUT FIRST, AND WHY
The least valuable line, not the least fun one. One sentence on why.
Common mistake: nothing marked CUT. A plan with nothing cut has not been prioritised. About 20 hours per person per week is this course's working limit, not a syllabus rule.
